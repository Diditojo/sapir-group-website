02 - Executive Summary

A capital plan calibrated to three operator archetypes

Premium longevity is a fragmenting category. The capital required to credibly compete depends almost entirely on which archetype the operator chooses to occupy — full-medical (Lanserhof / Clinique La Prairie), medical-light (SHA / Chenot), or wellness-plus (Six Senses Place). The equipment-only spread between the lightest and heaviest credible build is roughly 3x, before any Russia-specific landed-cost adjustment is applied.

Across a 90-key Sysert build, equipment capex ranges from approximately EUR 4.4M (Six Senses tier) to EUR 13.7M (Lanserhof / CLP tier) at Western European list prices. Russia delivered cost is materially higher: 30-50% premium driven by parallel-import routing, customs and VAT, and FX volatility — pushing the full-medical build to roughly EUR 19-20M equivalent on landed basis. North America runs 15-20% above Europe; Asia (Singapore / China hub) runs 10-15% below.

The strategic recommendation is sequenced: launch on a SHA / Chenot tier envelope (EUR 6.6M EU equivalent) with diagnostic imaging limited to ultrasound, DEXA, and X-ray; outsource MRI / CT through a partner relationship in Yekaterinburg; reserve approximately EUR 6-7M of contingency to step up to full Lanserhof depth in years 3-5 if guest mix and ADR support it. This protects payback while preserving optionality.
